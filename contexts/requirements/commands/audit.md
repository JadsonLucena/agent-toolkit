# Audit

## Purpose

Entry point for evaluating requirement quality before downstream planning or implementation.

## Uses

* Agent: `contexts/requirements/agents/requirements-specifier.md`
* Skill: `contexts/requirements/skills/validation/skill.md`
* Rule: `contexts/requirements/rules/requirements.md`

## Inputs

A requirement specification, its supporting evidence, and the intended downstream use.

## Result

A status of `sufficient`, `needs-revision`, `needs-clarification`, or `blocked`, with traceable material findings and unresolved questions.
