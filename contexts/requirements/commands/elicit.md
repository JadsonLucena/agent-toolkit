# Elicit

## Purpose

Entry point for discovering and structuring stakeholder needs before formal requirement definition.

## Uses

* Agent: `contexts/requirements/agents/requirements-elicitor.md`
* Skill: `contexts/requirements/skills/elicitation/skill.md`
* Rule: `contexts/requirements/rules/requirements.md`

## Inputs

Available stakeholder context, documents, conversations, existing-system evidence, constraints, and known questions.

## Result

Structured elicitation evidence with goals, needs, stakeholders, constraints, business rules, assumptions, provenance, conflicts, risks, and open questions.
